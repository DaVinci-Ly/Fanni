About us

A Libyan importer of workshop equipment, generators, spare parts and building materials, based in Tripoli.

Who we are

Al-Mutakhassis Al-Fanni

A Libyan limited liability company, founded in Tripoli in 2024, that imports heavy machinery and equipment, their supplies and spare parts.

We work with workshops, contractors, and owners of generators and machinery. Someone who needs a single part gets the same attention as someone ordering in bulk.

And we tell you plainly what’s in stock now, and what will take time to arrive.

Licensed activity: import of heavy machinery and equipment, their supplies and spare parts.
